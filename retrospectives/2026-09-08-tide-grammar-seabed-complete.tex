\section{What ``complete'' means here}
Astra~\#36's wording is adopted verbatim in \texttt{HEADLINES.md}: the Lean formalisation establishes
the fluctuation-function and ladder-algebra results, the Taylor-tree expansion in every positive normal
dimension under the declared analytic hypotheses, a continuous-coefficient finite-cutoff stochastic
extension through tangential integration and finite chart assembly, and the leading-order posterior
quotient under joint data convergence, source predecessor cancellation, negligible decomposition
residuals and an almost-surely nonzero limiting denominator coefficient. The stochastic results are
conditional on convergence in the stated weighted-$\ell^1$/tangential-data topologies and on an
externally supplied finite-chart decomposition; they do not derive those inputs from Hypothesis~I,
construct a resolution atlas, or identify a Gaussian empirical-process limit. All-orders posterior
division and the population expansion in the unrevised \S3 remain outside the release. ``Complete''
is a release decision about a delimited scope, not a claim that every paper label has a Lean
counterpart.

\section{The closing corollaries}
The different-scale quotient is the generic quotient theorem applied to the normalised pair and the
identity $(U/a)/(V/b)=(b/a)(U/V)$ for $a,b\ne0$; the assembled form needs the pair of assembled
remainders at two \emph{different} targets, a two-target copy of the uniform-on-balls pair lemma. The
deterministic bridge is the uniform-on-balls global remainder theorem evaluated at one point
(\texttt{TendstoUniformlyOn.tendsto\_at}), plus the residual and, for the equivalence,
\texttt{isEquivalent\_of\_tendsto\_one} (one explicit argument at this pin).

\section{Lessons of the programme}
Nineteen units, five reviews, no mathematical defect found: the recipe was a frozen deterministic
core, an abstract bookkeeping layer (finite-cutoff expansions), Mathlib's convergence-in-distribution
API used as-is, and a gate after every third unit. The recurring tooling lesson is mechanical: gate
every commit on the build, the sorry audit and the line-length linter, and keep hypothesis names away
from \texttt{variable} names.
