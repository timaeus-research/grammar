\section{What changed in the mathematics}
The paper states $\mathbb E[K]=\lambda/n-(m-1)/(n\log n)+o(1/(n\log n))$ and obtains it by
differentiating the asymptotic expansion of $\mathcal Z_n$ in $n$. That step is not justified in
general, and review v38 forbade it. The formal route instead transports coefficients: the energy
insertion is the exact weight shift $h\mapsto h+2k$ (an identity of integrands), the state-density
representation commutes with the unit shift of its weights, and the Mellin moment recurrence
$M(\mu+1,i)=(\mu M(\mu,i)-iM(\mu,i-1))/\beta$ turns into the coefficient recurrence
$C_K(\mu+1,j)=(\mu C(\mu,j)-(j+1)C(\mu,j+1))/\beta$. With $C(\lambda,q)=0$ above $m-1$ this gives
$A_K=\lambda A/\beta$ and $B_K=(\lambda B-(m-1)A)/\beta$ for the two leading coefficients, and the
quotient algebra $N L(\mathcal Z_K/\mathcal Z-\lambda/(\beta N))=(A(B_K+\varepsilon_K)-A_K(B+\varepsilon))/(A(A+(B+\varepsilon)/L))$
on an eventual set where $\mathcal Z\neq0$ closes the theorem. The remainder control needed at the
next-log scale, and the log-weighted control needed for $m=1$, both come from the same positive
power gap $\lambda+1/Q>\lambda$ of the cutoff expansion; convergence at the leading scale alone
would not have sufficed, and the exact energy identity $\mathcal Z_K=-(1/\beta)\mathcal Z'$ is
proved as a theorem about integrals but is never used to differentiate an expansion.

Selection after cancellation (N1) is a finite combinatorial statement about the admissible index set
$\Lambda\cap[0,L]\times\{0,\dots,D\}$ under the order ``smaller exponent, then larger log degree'':
a globally first nonzero pair exists uniquely whenever any coefficient is nonzero, and a cutoff
expansion is asymptotically equivalent to that term; when every admissible coefficient vanishes the
integral is $o(N^{-L})$ for every $L$, which does not make it zero ($e^{-N}$ is flat and nonzero).

Analytic families (N3) close a hypothesis Programme P had left open: a family $F_v$ holomorphic on a
common polydisc of radius $R>1$, jointly continuous and bounded on a smaller radius $1<r<R$,
defines continuous zero-noise data in the weighted-$\ell^1$ topology (the geometric majorant
$Mr^{-|\gamma|}$ dominates the Cauchy coefficients uniformly), so the tangential integration and
assembly theorems of Programme P apply with $\operatorname{Re}F_v$ as the represented amplitude.

Positive boxes (N4) are an exact dilation $\mathcal Z_b(N;\eta)=b^{|h|+d}\mathcal Z_1(Nb^{2|k|};\eta(b\,\cdot))$,
so the leading coefficient on $(0,b]^d$ is $b^{|h|+d-2|k|\lambda}A(\eta(b\,\cdot))$ and the lower
log coefficients are the binomial re-expansion of $\log N+2|k|\log b$ already built into the box
coefficient of Headline XXVIII.

\section{Lessons}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Transcribe interfaces from the file, not from memory.} The one blocking item in the
release audit was a minus sign I typed into the frozen-interface block of the review prompt; the
Lean definition has the positive shift. The reviewer could not see the repository and rightly held
the release until the sign was reconciled. Copy definitions with \texttt{grep}.
\item \emph{The remainder scale is part of the statement.} Review v39 distinguished three scales
(leading, next-log, log-weighted) and the proof needed the stronger two. Generalising
\texttt{population\_remainder\_tendsto} to divide by $L^j$ and to multiply by $L^j$ cost one lemma
each because the underlying estimate was a power gap; had it been a leading-scale limit only, the
theorem would have been unprovable from it.
\item \emph{\texttt{set} shadows terms introduced later.} Abbreviations made with \texttt{set}
before a \texttt{rw \dots\ at} broke \texttt{div\_self} and \texttt{mul\_div\_cancel\_left₀} three
times; fold with \texttt{simp only [← hdef]} or avoid \texttt{set} in algebraic closers.
\item \emph{Integral notation swallows subtraction.} \verb|∫ …, a - b * ∫ …| parses the tail into the
integrand; parenthesise the first integral.
\item \emph{Root-build name clashes are silent until the full build.} Two clashes
(\texttt{clampCube}, \texttt{population\_coeff\_eq\_zero\_of\_lt}) passed per-module builds; the
exit-gated full build before every push caught both.
\item \emph{Do not restate a lemma under new names to spend budget.} The assembled energy correction
was left out on the reviewer's advice: the missing pieces are the paired numerator/denominator
assembled coefficient identification and the assembly bridges, not residual decay, and a wrapper
would have proved nothing new.
\end{itemize}
