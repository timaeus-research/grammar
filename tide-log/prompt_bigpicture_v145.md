## Paper text now in grammar_lean_2.tex §9
\paragraph{Stability in the field.} … K_p compact chart image, R_p(μ)=Σ_i(2k_i L_μ − h_i) canonical depth …
\begin{thm}[Continuity of the coefficients in the branch representatives] Fix the resolved data, c ≥ 1, μ > 0 and F vanishing near D_{c+1}. Let ψ_n, ψ be smooth root fields whose branch representatives converge on every piece, L_{p,n} → L_p in C^{R_p(μ)} uniformly on K_p. Then C^emp_{μ,c−1}(F;ψ_n) → C^emp_{μ,c−1}(F;ψ). \end{thm}
"…The statement is for a fixed index: no single order serves the whole expansion, the approximants are smooth, the geometry and the observable are fixed, and no agreement of the branch representatives across the walls is imposed. It is the deterministic input for random-field statements about the subleading coefficients, which need in addition convergence in distribution of the branch representatives in this finite-order topology."
Non-claims: "The coefficients are continuous in the branch representatives for the finite-order topology of Theorem [empcontinuity]. Not formalised are the random-field convergence of the subleading coefficients (which needs, in addition, convergence in distribution of the branch representatives in that topology), and the identification of the lower logarithmic weights with derivatives of S_ν in the exponent."
