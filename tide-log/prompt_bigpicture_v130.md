## 2. The proposal: GRADED stratum integrals (canonical, no regularisation)
Observation. Resonance is EXACT: `Resonates μ (k,h) := ∃ n : ℕ, 2kμ = h+1+n`, and the `n` is precisely the Taylor order `m_j` of the face term that contributes at exponent `μ` (`faceCoef k β b J (m+h) μ j ≠ 0` forces `2k_jμ = m_j + h_j + 1` for every `j ∈ J`). So at a face `J` of size `c` all of whose walls resonate with `μ`, exactly ONE Taylor multi-order `α_J` with `α_j = 2k_jμ − h_j − 1` survives, and for the top log power `q = c − 1 = DJ` the binomial sum collapses to `j = c − 1`, `e = j − q = 0`: NO log factor in the face weight.

Unit E1 (engine, M). `smoothCoeff_eq_faceSum_of_vanishing`: if `F` vanishes on a neighbourhood (in the closed box) of every face of size `≥ c+1` (equivalently near `{v : at least c+1 coordinates vanish}`), then
  smoothCoeff F h k β b μ (c−1) = ∑_{J : |J| = c, ∀ j∈J resonant at μ} faceW J α_J · faceCoef k β b J (α_J + h) μ (c−1) · ∫_{(0,b]^K} (∂_J^{α_J} F)(0_J, w) · ∏_{i∈K} w_i^{h_i} (∏_{i∈K} w_i^{2k_i})^{−μ} dw.
  Ingredients all exist: faces of size ≥ c+1 die by `faceAmp_eq_zero_of_jets_zero`; faces of size < c die by `faceCoef_eq_zero_of_resonantCount_le` (need j ≥ c−1 ≥ |J| > DJ); at size c only j = c−1; the Taylor remainder `remList` in the K-directions of `∂_J^α F` equals `∂_J^α F` because the K-jets of `∂_J^α F` vanish on `{u_J = 0, u_i = 0}` (depth ≥ c+1) — needs a lemma `remList_eq_self_of_jetsZero_on_hyperplanes`; the face integral is over a set on which the integrand vanishes near every `w_i = 0` (i active in K), hence an honest convergent integral (it is already an `∫` in Lean; no regularisation appears because the weight singularities are cut off by the vanishing of F).
Unit E2 (resolved, M–L). Define `𝓘_{c+1} := {F : ∀ᶠ P in 𝓝ˢ (Z₀ ∩ depthGE (c+1)), F P = 0}` and the STRATUM INTEGRAL `Σ_{μ,c}[F]` as the chart-piece sum of the E1 face integrals (over the transport). Theorem: for `F ∈ 𝓘_{c+1}`, `𝒯^U_{μ,c−1}[F] = Σ_{μ,c}[F]`. (Already known for `F ∈ 𝓘_{c+1}`: `𝒯^U_{μ,q}[F] = 0` for `q ≥ c`, CDXXXV.) Intrinsic reading: on observables vanishing near the deeper strata, the top-log-power coefficient at every exponent is a convergent integral over the depth-c μ-resonant stratum `Σ^μ_c := {depth = c, r_μ = c} ∩ Z₀` of the normal jet of order `α` of `F` against an explicit density; the pieces are chart pieces, but the total is intrinsic because 𝒯 is.
Unit E3 (S–M). Corollaries: if `α_J = 0` at every point of the stratum (μ is the COMMON ratio of the c walls, e.g. μ = λ* at depth m*, but also any depth-c stratum all of whose walls have ratio μ), the integrand is `ω|b|·prior∘π·F` and the constants `faceW = 1`, `faceCoef > 0` — POSITIVITY of `𝒯^U_{μ,c−1}` on `𝓘_{c+1} ∩ {F ≥ 0}`, and VALUES-ONLY dependence on `Σ^μ_c`. This generalises the leading-index positivity (CDXXXII/CDXXXIV) to every stratum: each stratum carries its own positive "leading" functional on observables vanishing near deeper strata.
Unit E4 (paper, S). The graded picture of the Euclidean coefficients `C_{μ,q}(f) = 𝒯^U_{μ,q}[f∘π]`: supports in `π(Z₀ ∩ {r_μ ≥ q+1})`; on `f∘π ∈ 𝓘_{c+1}` the `(μ,c−1)` coefficient is a stratum integral; the lower log powers `q < c−1` on `𝓘_{c+1}` are sums of face integrals over shallower faces whose boundaries reach the depth-c stratum where `F ≠ 0`, i.e. the power-log weights `mono a w^{−μ} logSum^{e}` are genuinely singular there — these are the "regularised strata integrals" and they are NOT canonical piecewise (the depth-p Taylor cutoff moves mass between a face and its boundary). We would state them as chart-piece formulas only.

## 3. Alternatives we considered
(B) Zeta/Mellin regularisation: every face integral has a meromorphic continuation in μ; the coefficients are Laurent data of `ζ_f(z) = ∫ φ f K^z` at `z = −μ`, canonical without chart choices — but needs the meromorphic continuation machinery on U (L–XL) and Astra #129 rated zeta-pole identification separately.
