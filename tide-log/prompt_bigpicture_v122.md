## 3. Observations we want you to check
(A) The paper's per-stratum expansion is a formal series in `r` (moment tensors depend on `n`); made precise at order `(μ,q)` only FINITELY many normal derivatives contribute (those `m` with `(m_i + h_i + 1)/(2k_i)` producing `μ`), and for a SMOOTH (non-analytic) density the "Σ'_r" must be read as this finite sum. (B) Since the total `c_{μ,q}` is intrinsic, ANY second cutoff expansion of the same integral identifies its coefficients with `c_{μ,q}` by uniqueness — so one route is: build the paper's decomposition (a stratum-adapted partition) as a SECOND expansion and invoke uniqueness, never reorganising the engine's remainder formula algebraically. (C) Stratum-adapted cutoffs (constant in normal directions) require compatible normal fibres across charts (the paper's tubular neighbourhoods, property (iv)); "constant along the active coordinates" in one chart is not preserved by chart transitions, so such cutoffs are a genuinely global construction on the resolved manifold — unless done CHART-LOCALLY (inside each chart box, after the chartwise bumps have localised the integral to the box): within one box, a Euclidean partition `Σ_{J ⊆ active} ρ_J(v) = 1` on the box with `ρ_J` constant in `v_J` near `v_J = 0` and supported where `v_K` is bounded away from `0` (deeper walls) IS constructible and needs no compatibility; applied to the chart integral `∫_{box} G v^h e^{−N v^{2k}}` it gives pieces `∫ ρ_J G …` each a smooth core with base `(s, v_K)` (compact, `v_K` away from `0`), active `J`, tangential unit `β(s,v_K) = β(s) v_K^{2k_K}` — to which the engine applies AGAIN (its own faces `J' ⊆ J` then appear, with remainders; the paper's `M_{I,r}(n)` = full fibre integral over `R^{|I|}` hides exactly this sub-face structure). So a chart-local adapted partition reproduces the paper's `Σ_I Z_n[φ; I]` decomposition but does not by itself remove the remainder structure at order `(μ,q)`. (D) The remainder structure is intrinsic to any rigorous version of `⟨D^r_⊥ φ, M_{I,r}(n)⟩` for smooth data: the moment tensor's own expansion in `n` has the tied-ratio log terms and the sub-face contributions.
