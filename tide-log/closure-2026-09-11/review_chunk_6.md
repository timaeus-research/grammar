## Summary

139 dots reviewed (all in the companion note `averaging_dataset.tex`); 127 are faithful as written, 12 are flagged. The two serious problems are (i) the compact-base bounds on $V_\rho$ in `prop:compact_base`(2), where the prose attributes the lower bound $V_\rho\ge0$ to positivity of the bilocal form and the upper bound to AM–GM, whereas the Lean (with `compactV = compactDiag − compactQ`) proves exactly the reverse, so both dots `compactQ_nonneg` and `compactQnum_le` certify the opposite inequality and `compactV_le` is dotted for a two-sided bound it does not state (the lower bound `compactV_nonneg` exists but is undotted); and (ii) the "leading posterior is random" paragraph, where `spatialPhase_tendsto` (leading term of the phase integral) is cited for posterior energy-law convergence and location concentration, which are `spatialEnergyLaw_tendsto` / `phasePosterior_tendsto_faceLocation` with extra positivity hypotheses. The systematic pattern in the assembly section is that late "Hence/only/conditional only on" sentences silently drop standing hypotheses (the threshold $p\beta M_0^2<2$ or $p\beta\kappa<2$, the zero-phase amplitude datum, Bochner integrability of the observation, the distributional-limit hypothesis $A_nZ_n\Rightarrow L$), and two exponent-identification dots gloss over Lean's truncated-subtraction conclusion ($m\ge1$) and the local hypotheses on the analytic phase ($K(w)=0$, not locally flat, Lebesgue prior). The Gaussian-quartet, Stein, covariance-interpolation, compact-base Gaussian, annealed-identity, expectation-bridge, uniform-moment, box-moment, Fernique/certificate, and endpoint dots all match their Lean statements, including constants ($K_{\mathrm{face}}=1/((m-1)!\prod_{\text{leading}}k_i)$, $c=b^{2|k|}$, the $1/2$ in $J_{2\lambda}=S_\lambda/2$, and the reduced temperature $\beta(1-p\beta c/2)$).
