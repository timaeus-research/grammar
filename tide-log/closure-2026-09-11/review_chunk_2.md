## Summary

141 dots reviewed (mirror L1095–L1198: coordinate-stratum bundle, Programmes P/Q population theorems, the geometric bridge and hironaka adapter, energy/free-energy hierarchy, constant-phase and phase-law material). 119 are faithful; 22 are flagged above (several as grouped entries), of which three are substantive OVER-CLAIM/MISLINK cases where a dot sits on a *printed display* that the Lean either corrects or contradicts: \eqref{eq:lambda_I_f} (`population_shift_leadingCoeff` — first candidate, not leading exponent; disproved by `signed_cancellation`), \eqref{eq:thm_leading_coeff} (`population_leadingCoeff` — face functional vs printed corner evaluation; disproved by `face_dependence`), and the Example display $\mathbb E[K]\sim\lambda/n$ (`energy_ratio_tendsto` — chart level, nonzero face functional). Two are pure relinks (`foot_tubeMap`→`tubeMap_zero`; `isEquivalent_first_nonzero_of_cutoffExpansion`→`existsUnique_first_nonzero_isEquivalent`). The systematic pattern in the remainder is silently supplied hypotheses of two kinds: (i) finiteness/nonnegativity of measures and observables in the Laplace-exponent and localisation material (`[IsFiniteMeasure μ]` section variables, `obs ≥ 0`, nonnegative partition weights, moment domination `hdom`), and (ii) the nonvanishing/positivity of the leading coefficient ($A\ne0$, $A>0$ at every phase) in the assembled Gamma-Laplace, free-energy and moving-phase statements, which the prose states early in the paragraph and then drops. One notational hazard: the paragraph at L1198 overloads $J$ ($J_{\nu,i}$ = `fluctMoment` in the $t$ variable vs $J_{2\lambda}$ = `phaseMoment` in the $s=\sqrt t$ variable, differing by a factor 2 and an index doubling), harmless in ratios but misleading in the formula for $A(a)$.
