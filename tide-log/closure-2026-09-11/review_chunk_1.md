## Summary

141 dots reviewed; 121 judged faithful, 20 dots flagged in 16 entries (several entries cover multiple dots on one sentence). No wrong-theorem links of the "says something else entirely" kind except `weightComponent_eq` (a bookkeeping lemma) and `corner_evaluation_fails` (inequality cited for a limit value, though the correct theorem exists two declarations away). The systematic pattern is in the geometric §4 material (L654–L759): the Lean is deliberately **pointwise / linear-frame / Euclidean-additive-family** (ConormalSplitting, SymmetricWeights, StrucDual tube), while the mirror sentences it decorates are stated for bundles over a general submanifold of a manifold — the L691 Lean remark says this honestly, but the dots on the main-text sentences at L654, L660, L674, L694, L759 do not carry the qualifier. Two hypothesis omissions recur: analyticity of fibre restrictions (L674) and finite-dimensionality of the ambient inner product space (L691). The headline/Programme-P-Q dots (L924–L1095) are in good shape; constants, normalisations (`N^{-2l}=N^{-p}`, `b`-factors, `(d-1)! = m!`) and kernels (`ncKernel`, `quadKernel`) all match, with only the L879 display's `1/r!` and the "exactly when" sub-Gaussian threshold as substantive slips.
