## 2. The gap: from `WatanabeModificationOn` (a manifold `U`, proper `g`, LOCAL charts at points) to a `DomainSectorAtlas` (FINITELY many box charts in ℝ^d with a.e.-DISJOINT images, exact box domains, tangential units, boundary monomials) and then to `SheetInputs`

My reading of the obstacles, please correct:
(a) **Finiteness and boxes.** For a compact `K₀ ⊆ W` (the compact support region), `g⁻¹ K₀` is compact (properness INTO `W`); the Watanabe charts at points over `f⁻¹0` and the iso `g` off the zero set give an open cover; extract a finite subcover; shrink each chart target to a closed box `[−a_i, a_i]^d ⊆ φ.target` (in the chart coordinates the phase is exactly `∏ u^{2k}` and the Jacobian `b · u^h`). Off the zero set, charts of `g⁻¹` (identity charts in ℝ^d) have `k = 0`, phase unit `= f` itself (positive), inert everywhere — these are the "empty active set" pieces (all tail).
(b) **A.e.-disjointness of the chart images (the real obstacle).** The box images `g(φ_i⁻¹ [−a_i,a_i]^d)` OVERLAP.
