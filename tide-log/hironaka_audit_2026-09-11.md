## The `Q(n)` refinement step

The refinement step is `exists_qResolution_of_pointwise` (RestartAssembly.lean:191) and its sibling
`exists_qResolution_of_restart_resolution` (RestartAssembly.lean:147), used by
`exists_qResolution_of_adaptedRep_aux` and `exists_qResolution_at_stepC_point` (QInduction.lean). Given
`R : PartialResolution n Δ.f N` and, for every chart `i` and every point `z ∈ R.dom i`, a compact
neighbourhood `Nz ∈ 𝓝 z` with a `PartialResolution n (T i).f Nz` whose charts composed with `R.φ i`
are `IsQChart`, it produces `R' := R.comp S` where `S i` is obtained by
`PartialResolution.ofLocal_forallChart (R.dom_compact i)`: a finite subcover of `R.dom i` by
the `Nz`, then `sigma` over the chosen points, then `restrict` to `R.dom i`.

Consequences, by inspection of the three constructors:

* **Preserved**: coverage a.e. (`comp.cover`, `sigma.cover`, `restrict.cover`), compactness of
  domains, smoothness on open neighbourhoods, chartwise injectivity off the composite exceptional
  set `E p := (S p.1).E p.2 ∪ ((S p.1).dom p.2 ∩ (S p.1).φ p.2 ⁻¹' R.E p.1)`, nonvanishing Jacobian,
  a *finite* multiplicity bound, and the per-chart predicate `IsQChart` (through `ChartPredStable`
  and `forallChart_sigma`/`forallChart_restrict`). This is exactly "coverage and monomialisation".
* **Not preserved / never present**: (i) global overlap information — the inner families `S i`,
  `S i'` for different outer charts are built independently, and within one `S i` the summands of the
  `sigma` are local resolutions of *overlapping* neighbourhoods `Nz`, so the refined family is
  genuinely many-to-one on positive-measure sets; `comp.mult` multiplies (`R.mult * sup_i (S i).mult`)
  and `sigma.mult` adds, so the recorded bound grows geometrically with the depth of the induction
  and no equality is tracked; (ii) ownership — `restrict` clips each summand's domain to
  `dom ∩ φ ⁻¹' (R.dom i)`, which makes summand images overlap on `R.dom i` rather than partition it;
  no disjointification (e.g. `Nz₁ \ Nz₂`) is performed; (iii) the admissible composites `π` inside
  `IsQChart` are prepended chartwise (`IsQChart.of_move`, composing `R.φ i ∘ S.φ j`) with no
  statement relating the composites of different charts.

So: replacing a chart by a partial resolution of the pulled-back function preserves coverage (a.e.)
and monomialisation only. It does not preserve, and the induction never establishes, overlap or
ownership information; the honest statement is that the output of `Q_all` is a finite family of
independent monomial charts with a common *target* `N` and a summed/multiplied multiplicity bound.

## Answer pattern

Result: **no to 1, no to 2, partial to 3, no to 4, no to 5.** Under the classification given, "no to
1 or 2" applies: the chartwise `Q(n)` output is **not a resolution atlas**. It is a finite family of
admissible composites into the target, each with its own `π_i` and its own final analytic local
isomorphism `ψ_i`, glued only by the measure-theoretic clauses `cover`/`mult_bound` of
`PartialResolution`; there is no common source space, no transition maps for the composite charts
(the only transition map in the repository, `blowUpTransition`, is for one blow-up, is defined only
off the exceptional hyperplane, and is not used in the `Q(n)` proof), and the multiplicity is
honestly `> 1` on positive-measure sets after each `ofLocal` gluing. The extra global
coverage/integration infrastructure of "no to 3 or 4" is therefore also needed (an exact cover is
present in the primitive constructors but is dropped by the structure; a multiplicity function or an
ownership partition is absent), and the tiling theorem of "no to 5" is genuinely new mathematics
relative to what is formalised. The separate, globally glued blow-up of the manifold strand
(`Monomialize.Manifold.BlowUpGlue`, `GluedBlowDown`) and the Watanabe-form readout
(`Monomialize/Manifold/Watanabe/Readout.lean`, exact cover and global injectivity off `f⁻¹(0)`, but
its records go through `BOanFamAll` with `sorryAx` per its docstring) are the places in the
repository where a common resolved object exists; neither is connected to `Q_all` or
`exists_monomialResolution_at`, so for `grammar` they are pointers, not substrate.
