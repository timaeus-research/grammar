## 2. What a certificate demands (grammar side; fields verbatim in spirit)

`ResolvedCertificate R D W K ϕ φ` over a `ResolvedGeometry R` (space U, proper π : U → ℝ^d, finitely many divisor components E_i closed, exponents k_i, h_i; strata S_I = points lying on exactly the components in I) and `ResolvedNormalData D` (normal spaces N_I(s) ⊆ A of dimension |I|, tubular germs Φ_I(s, ·) : N_I(s) → U):
- a localisation datum L: measure μ on U with `transport : μ.map π = (volume.restrict W).withDensity (ofReal ∘ ϕ)` (EXACT), phase K∘π, observable φ∘π, a gap δ;
- finitely many cores k = 1..M, each a `CorePresentation`: compact base K_k (in the stratum S_{I_k}), finite measure ν_k on K_k, exponents h, k (all k_i > 0), box side b, chart map Φ_k : K_k × ℝ^{n_k+1} → U (measurable), density c_k ≥ 0, tangential datum x_k ∈ C(K_k, ℓ¹-weighted coefficient space), with
  `transport_k : ((ν_k ⊗ Lebesgue on the box).withDensity (∏|u_i|^{h_i} · c_k)).map Φ_k = μ restricted to the core`,
  `phase_normal : K(π(Φ_k(s,u))) = β ∏ u_i^{2k_i}` a.e. (EXACT, β a constant — no unit),
  `amplitude_eq : (the power series with coefficients x_k(s)) evaluated at u = c_k(s,u) · φ(π(Φ_k(s,u)))` a.e. — i.e. the AMPLITUDE IS A CONVERGENT ANALYTIC POWER SERIES IN THE NORMAL VARIABLES u, with coefficients merely CONTINUOUS in the base point s;
- a tail: μ minus the cores has phase ≥ δ > 0 a.e. (contributes O(e^{−nδ}));
- normal-moment presentations (radius, ℓ¹ bounds), frames N_I(s) ≃ ℝ^{n_k+1}, and `Φ_eq : Φ_k(s,u) = tubular germ along the frame`.
`CoefficientCertificate` then follows from existing producers (phases 3/G: holomorphic-extension packets → face series).
Cores of a decomposition must be a.e.-DISJOINT in U (they partition μ). The engine's coefficient at a power–log index is an ℓ¹-sum over the whole normal series (for product phases ∏u_i^{2k_i} a monomial u_2^N contributes at the LEADING exponent (h_1+1)/(2k_1) — "infinite normal order at codim ≥ 2", consult #104 J4), so a naive Taylor truncation in u of a merely smooth amplitude does NOT give an o(n^{−A}) remainder: the remainder's u_2-behaviour is again a pole problem.

## 4. The obstruction, stated precisely

Grammar's engine wants a.e.-disjoint product cores with amplitudes analytic in u. Hironaka gives overlapping analytic charts. Smooth partition-of-unity weights (Daniel's branch, Watanabe's route in the book) make the amplitude non-analytic in u. Product cores from DIFFERENT charts over adjacent base pieces of the same stratum overlap in wedges of positive measure (normal fibrations differ between adapted charts: u' = u·unit, s' = s'(s,u)). The cube avoided all of this because the sectors {|y_β| ≥ |y_j|} are exact chart boxes globally. Note what the engine DOES allow: dependence on the base point s that is only continuous (TangentialData = C(K, ℓ¹)); cutoffs in s alone are free; the chart map Φ_k need only be measurable with the analytic-in-u amplitude identity.

R1 Compatible tubular structure: on the resolved manifold (or on the disjoint union of chart boxes with identifications), a system of normal fibrations of neighbourhoods of the strata, compatible across strata (control-data style), with fibres analytic and base dependence continuous; adapted charts whose normal coordinates are the fibre coordinates; then base-only cutoffs/partitions make cores of different charts over a.e.-disjoint base pieces a.e.-disjoint, and water-filling (CCCXXII–XXIV) handles the corners within a chart. Concern: the induction is designed with overlaps; redesign cost.

Concern: the iterated-pole problem above; a parallel development of the whole moment-representation layer.

R5 Multiplicity-corrected transport: exact identity ∑_i (pushforwards) = m(y)·prior with m the (piecewise-constant, integer) multiplicity, and an engine that expands ∫ m φ ϕ e^{−nK} instead — is there a way to divide the multiplicity out at the level of coefficients?
